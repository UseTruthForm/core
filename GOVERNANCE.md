# Governance

TruthForm is maintained by Sallux, which started it. The maintainers are in
[MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md).

## Decisions

- **Day to day,** a pull request merges once a maintainer approves it and CI
  passes. A maintainer does not approve their own.
- **Larger changes** (a new platform, a change to the snapshot format, to
  what a capture withholds, or to the [rules TruthForm keeps](README.md#rules-it-keeps))
  start as an issue. They go ahead when no maintainer objects within a week
  of the proposal being complete. An objection says what would resolve it.
- **When maintainers disagree** and talking does not settle it, the
  maintainers vote, with a simple majority. Until there are three
  maintainers, Sallux decides.

## Becoming a maintainer

Anyone who has contributed steadily for a few months (code, reviews,
documentation or triage) can be nominated by a maintainer. The other
maintainers agree by lazy consensus. A maintainer who has been inactive for
six months is moved to emeritus, and can come back by asking.

## Changing this document

Like a larger change: an issue, then a pull request that no maintainer
objects to within a week.
